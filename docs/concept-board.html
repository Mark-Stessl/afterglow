Concept board

Names, icon, colour palettes and the cover styles for the Nightshelf rebuild. Chosen: Afterglow, lit-building icon. The full plan (rooms, tech, build order) is in CONCEPT.md.

Name ideas

The overview is a building at night with lit rooms, so most of these play on night, film and stage.

My pick
Afterglow
The glow of lit windows at night, and the feeling after a good movie or a platinum. Easy to say in German and English.
Backlot
A film studio's lot where all the sets stand. Fits "five sceneries" exactly, a bit less known as a word.
Encore
"Once more", the same idea done again, better. Stage and cinema feel.
Late Show
Cinema and TV at night. Very clear, slightly long as an app name.
Nightcap
The last thing before bed. Playful, keeps "night" from Nightshelf.
Moonroom
A room you go to at night. Soft, short, own word.
Marquee
The lit sign over a cinema entrance. Strong for movies, weaker for manga.
Nightshelf
Keep the name, new app (v2). You lose nothing, but it carries the old feel.
